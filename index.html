How a booking works

Nothing is booked until you say yes.

Every booking on RealDays starts as a request. You see it, check your own calendar, and accept or decline. The customer's card is only charged once you've accepted. No double bookings, no surprises on a day you'd already filled, and no penalty for saying no.

  1. They find you.A customer searches, lands on your page and picks a date, a time and how many are coming.
  2. They send a request.They enter their details and card. Nothing is charged yet.
  3. You say yes or no.The request reaches you by email. Answer within 24 hours.
  4. It's confirmed.The customer is charged and gets a confirmation with your waiver and how to reach you. You get their name, phone and email.
  5. They show up.You run the experience the way you always do. Your money is paid the day after.

Need to move a date? Sort it with the customer directly and let us know. The money doesn't move.

Your money

Paid to your bank the day after the experience.

Payments run on Stripe, the same system behind Airbnb, Uber and Shopify. You open a Stripe account through RealDays in about ten minutes with your ABN and bank details. From then on, every accepted booking is held safely and paid to your account the day after the experience runs.

  • What you pay.Only the card fee, about 1.7% plus 30 cents a booking for Australian cards, until 1 May 2027. Nothing else.
  • Cancellations.Customers can cancel free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours, they can reschedule or forfeit. If you or the weather cancel, the customer gets a full refund.To confirm You can set a longer window for multi-day experiences.
  • Refunds.RealDays issues every refund within one business day, under the policy above. Because the money is held until after the experience, a refund never has to come back out of your bank.
  • Disputes.If a customer's bank ever queries a charge, RealDays pays the dispute fee and handles the paperwork. Because every booking is one you accepted, "I didn't authorise this" disputes almost never happen.

What we ask of you

Ten minutes a week. That's it.

RealDays only works if customers can trust a yes. In return for the bookings, we ask five things:

  1. Answer every request within 24 hours.A quick no is better than a slow yes.
  2. Tell us your quiet slots.The days you'd most like filled are the ones we'll push hardest.
  3. Tell us the moment anything changes.A cancelled day, a moved date, a new price. One message is enough.
  4. Send us your waiver and insurance once.We attach the waiver to every confirmation. We never ask again unless something changes.
  5. Treat RealDays customers as your own.They are. You have their details, and they'll come back to you directly.

Everything else, the listing, the photos, the marketing, the money, is ours to do.
