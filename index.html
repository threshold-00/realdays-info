The problem

People are searching for what to do in Victoria. They can’t find you.

The big marketplaces take 20 to 30% of every booking, keep your customer’s email, and list you on page six next to hundreds of city tours. The tourism sites list you for a yearly fee and never tell you what it sent. So the people doing the most interesting things in Victoria, the glassblower, the horse trail, the dawn kayak, stay a phone call or a buried Instagram away. Most people don’t make the call. They book what’s easy.

Search “Melbourne” on the big four. Pages of city tours, and the owner-run businesses nowhere in sight.

Your PAYMENTS

Payment guide

Payments run on Stripe, the same system behind Airbnb, Uber and Shopify, so your details and your customers’ details are kept secure. RealDays never sees card or account numbers. You open a Stripe account through RealDays in about ten minutes with your ABN and bank details. From then on, every accepted booking is held safely, sent to you the day after the experience, and in your bank one to two business days later.

Deposits.

You choose whether to take a deposit. If you do, the customer pays it when you accept the booking, and pays the balance from a link we email them a few days before. Anyone booking within 48 hours of the start pays in full. Both payments are sent to you the day after the experience.

Rescheduling.

If a customer asks to move and you agree, the booking and everything they’ve paid moves to the new session, and your payout follows the new date. While this is being built into the site, get in touch and we’ll move it for you.

Cancellations.

Customers can cancel free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours, they can reschedule or forfeit. If you or the weather cause a cancel, the customer gets a full refund.

You can set a longer window for multi-day experiences.

Refunds.

RealDays issues every refund within one business day, under the policy above. Because the money is held until after the experience, a refund never has to come back out of your bank.

Disputes.

If a customer's bank ever queries a charge, RealDays pays the dispute fee and handles the paperwork. Because every booking is one you accepted, "I didn't authorise this" disputes almost never happen.

What we ask of you

Ten minutes a week. That’s it.

RealDays only works if customers can trust a yes. In return for the bookings, we ask six things:

  1. 1

    Answer every request within 24 hours. A quick no is better than a slow yes.

  2. 2

    Tell us what you want to promote. An offer, a new experience, a season you’d like filled. That’s what we push.

  3. 3

    Tell us how you take bookings today. Your system, your calendar, your rules, so we fit around it rather than over it.

  4. 4

    Tell us the moment anything changes. A cancelled day, a moved date, a new price. One message is enough.

  5. 5

    Send us your waiver and insurance once. We attach the waiver to every confirmation. We never ask again unless something changes.

  6. 6

    Treat RealDays customers as your own. They are. You have their details, and they’ll come back to you directly.

Everything else, the listing, the photos, the marketing, the money, is ours to do.
