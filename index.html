What it costs

Free until May 2027. Then 12% on the bookings we bring you.

Nothing to join. Nothing a month. Until 1 May 2027, you pay only the card fee on each booking, about 1.7% plus 30 cents for Australian cards, the same as taking a card at the counter. From 1 May 2027, it's 12% of each booking made through RealDays, with GST and the card fee included. If a booking is refunded in full, so is our fee.

Your money

Paid out the day after the experience.

Payments run on Stripe, the same system behind Airbnb, Uber and Shopify. You open a Stripe account through RealDays in about ten minutes with your ABN and bank details. From then on, every accepted booking is held safely and sent to you the day after the experience runs. Stripe then pays it into your bank on its normal payout timing.

  • What you pay.Only the card fee, about 1.7% plus 30 cents a booking for Australian cards, until 1 May 2027. Nothing else.
  • Cancellations.Customers can cancel free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours, they can reschedule or forfeit. If you or the weather cancel, the customer gets a full refund.To confirm You can set a longer window for multi-day experiences.
  • Refunds.RealDays issues every refund within one business day, under the policy above. Because the money is held until after the experience, a refund never has to come back out of your bank.
  • Disputes.If a customer's bank ever queries a charge, RealDays pays the dispute fee and handles the paperwork. Because every booking is one you accepted, "I didn't authorise this" disputes almost never happen.

What we ask of you

Ten minutes a week. That's it.

RealDays only works if customers can trust a yes. In return for the bookings, we ask four things:

  1. Answer every request within 24 hours.A quick no is better than a slow yes.
  2. Tell us the moment anything changes.A cancelled day, a moved date, a new price. One message is enough.
  3. Send us any waivers.We'll include them in the customer's confirmation email.
  4. Treat RealDays customers as your own.They are. You have their details, and they'll come back to you directly.

Everything else, the listing, the photos, the marketing, the money, is ours to do.
